Training rules

Every week

  • Keep 1–3 reps in reserve on most strength sets.
  • Rest 90–120 sec on big lifts, 45–75 sec on smaller ones.
  • Log the heaviest set you managed. One number per exercise.
  • Hold or slowly add weight while dieting. Holding is a win.
  • Incline walking should feel brisk but sustainable, never wrecking.
  • One HIIT session a week to start. Add a second only once that feels easy.
Back up your data

Your logs are saved in this phone's browser, not on GitHub. Tap Export every week or so and keep the file somewhere safe. If you change phone or clear Safari, Import restores it.
